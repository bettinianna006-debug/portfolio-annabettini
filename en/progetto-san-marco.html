San Marco Pizzeria Arcade

A video-game-style questionnaire to help you choose a pizza. Experimental project, not in use.

Type
Web · Interactive quiz
Context
Experimental project, not in use
Skills
  • Interactive quiz
  • Artificial intelligence

The idea

In the beginning, while I was figuring out how to use artificial intelligence, I came up with a questionnaire to help choose a pizza: a few questions to work out which pizzas you might like most.

The game

To make it fun I turned it into a level-based video game, in 8-bit style. The opening, “Panico da menù?” (“Menu panic?”), speaks to anyone who freezes at the choice (the waiter waiting, friends who have already decided) and invites them to trust the algorithm.

Each level is a question, for example “Se c’è della carne, come la vuoi?” (“If there is meat, how do you want it?”) at level 5 of 10. At the end “Level cleared!” appears with the suggestion, the ingredients, other unlocked options and a button to play again.

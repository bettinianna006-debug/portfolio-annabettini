The context

Sperimentando is Padova’s main science exhibition. For the 2025 edition, on the theme “water”, the call was open to all the city’s schools.

The concept

I linked the fluidity of water to the gesture of knitting — two processes that share patience, rhythm and invisible interlacing. The resulting graphic mark is both poetic and scientific: a liquid texture that is woven like a thread, evoking methodical rigour and natural beauty.
